The details

How it's made

Your photos don't move. These do.

The clips are generated, not filmed

A listing photo is a still image. AI turns it into a moving shot, so the photo of the kitchen becomes a slow push across the kitchen, and five or ten of those cut together into a reel. That is the part you are paying for. It is also why there is no camera, no shoot and no day of anyone's time, and why I only need the address.

The words are copied off you

Before anything gets written I read your last posts and your listing write-ups. The captions and the text on screen are then written to match how you already sound, so the pack goes out reading like you wrote it rather than like a robot did. You get every caption as text, ready to paste.
